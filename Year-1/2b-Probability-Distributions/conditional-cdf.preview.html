Conditional CDF¶

Definition¶

Conditional CDF: for continuous X,YX,YX,Y and fY(y)>0,f_Y(y)>0,fY​(y)>0,

FX∣Y(x∣y)=∫−∞xfX∣Y(t∣y) dt.\begin{align*} F_{X\mid Y}\rb{x\given y} =\int_{-\infty}^x f_{X\mid Y}\rb{t\given y}\,\d t. \end{align*}FX∣Y​(x∣y)=∫−∞x​fX∣Y​(t∣y)dt.​

fX∣Y(t∣y)=fX,Y(t,y)fY(y).\begin{align*} f_{X\mid Y}\rb{t\given y}=\frac{f_{X,Y}(t,y)}{f_Y(y)}. \end{align*}fX∣Y​(t∣y)=fY​(y)fX,Y​(t,y)​.​
