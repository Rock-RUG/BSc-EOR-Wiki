Law of Total Expectation¶

Definition¶

Law of total expectation: If {Ai}\{A_i\}{Ai​} is a partition of the sample space with P(()Ai)>0\P(A_i)>0P(()Ai​)>0 for the conditioning events used and YYY is integrable, then

E[Y]=∑iE[Y∣Ai] P(Ai).\begin{align*} \E{Y}=\sum_i\E{Y\mid A_i}\,\P{A_{i}}. \end{align*}E[Y]=i∑​E[Y∣Ai​]P(Ai​).​

LOTP is a special case of LOTE.
