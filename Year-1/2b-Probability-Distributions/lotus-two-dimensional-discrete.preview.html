Two-Dimensional LOTUS for Discrete Variables¶

Theorem¶

For a discrete pair (X,Y)(X,Y)(X,Y) and a function ggg with ∑x,y∣g(x,y)∣pX,Y(x,y)<∞\sum_{x,y}|g(x,y)|p_{X,Y}(x,y)<\infty∑x,y​∣g(x,y)∣pX,Y​(x,y)<∞,

E[g(X,Y)]=∑x∑yg(x,y) pX,Y(x,y).\begin{aligned} \mathbb{E}[g(X,Y)]=\sum_x\sum_y g(x,y)\,p_{X,Y}(x,y). \end{aligned}E[g(X,Y)]=x∑​y∑​g(x,y)pX,Y​(x,y).​

Interpretation¶

A function of two variables can be averaged directly using their joint PMF, even when the variables are dependent.
