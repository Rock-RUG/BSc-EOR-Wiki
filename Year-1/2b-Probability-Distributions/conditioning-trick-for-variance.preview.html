Conditioning Trick for Variance¶

Method¶

When YYY has a natural conditional structure, for example Y∣X=x∼Pois⁡(x)Y\mid X=x\sim\operatorname{Pois}(x)Y∣X=x∼Pois(x), compute Var⁡(Y)\operatorname{Var}(Y)Var(Y) by decomposing it with Eve's law (total variance). This is a calculation method rather than a separate theorem; it needs E[Y2]<∞\mathbb{E}[Y^2]<\inftyE[Y2]<∞.

Interpretation¶

Choose information that makes a response’s mean and variance easy, then combine them using total variance. This avoids finding the marginal distribution.

For equally likely X=1X=1X=1 and X=3X=3X=3 with Y∣X∼Pois⁡(X)Y\mid X\sim\operatorname{Pois}(X)Y∣X∼Pois(X), the average conditional variance is (1+3)/2=2(1+3)/2=2(1+3)/2=2. The variance of the conditional means 111 and 333 is 111. The total variance is therefore Var⁡(Y)=2+1=3\operatorname{Var}(Y)=2+1=3Var(Y)=2+1=3, without first deriving the distribution of YYY.

Key Results¶

  • Compute m(X)=E[Y∣X]m(X)=\mathbb{E}[Y\mid X]m(X)=E[Y∣X] and v(X)=Var⁡(Y∣X)v(X)=\operatorname{Var}(Y\mid X)v(X)=Var(Y∣X) first; then Var⁡(Y)=E[v(X)]+Var⁡(m(X))\operatorname{Var}(Y)=\mathbb{E}[v(X)]+\operatorname{Var}(m(X))Var(Y)=E[v(X)]+Var(m(X)).
  • For Y∣X∼Pois⁡(X)Y\mid X\sim\operatorname{Pois}(X)Y∣X∼Pois(X) with finite second moment of XXX, EY=EX\mathbb{E}Y=\mathbb{E}XEY=EX and Var⁡(Y)=EX+Var⁡(X)\operatorname{Var}(Y)=\mathbb{E}X+\operatorname{Var}(X)Var(Y)=EX+Var(X).

Examples¶

  • If XXX is equally likely to be 111 or 333 and Y∣X∼Pois⁡(X)Y\mid X\sim\operatorname{Pois}(X)Y∣X∼Pois(X), then EY=2\mathbb{E}Y=2EY=2 and Var⁡(Y)=2+1=3\operatorname{Var}(Y)=2+1=3Var(Y)=2+1=3.
  • If X∼Bern⁡(1/2)X\sim\operatorname{Bern}(1/2)X∼Bern(1/2) and Y∣X∼Bin⁡(4,0.2+0.6X)Y\mid X\sim\operatorname{Bin}(4,0.2+0.6X)Y∣X∼Bin(4,0.2+0.6X), the within variance is 0.640.640.64, the conditional-mean variance is 1.441.441.44, and total variance is 2.082.082.08.
