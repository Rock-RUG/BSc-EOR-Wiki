    Sum of IID Uniforms¶

    Definition¶

    Sum of iid Uniforms: for X,Y∼iidUnif(0,1),X,Y\iidsim\Unif{0,1},X,Y∼iidUnif(0,1), X+YX+YX+Y has a triangular PDF on (0,2)(0,2)(0,2) peaking at 1.1.1.
