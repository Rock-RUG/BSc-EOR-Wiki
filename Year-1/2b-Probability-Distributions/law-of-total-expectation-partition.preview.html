Law of Total Expectation over a Partition¶

Theorem¶

If {Ai}\{A_i\}{Ai​} is a finite or countable partition of the sample space and YYY is integrable, then

E[Y]=∑iE[Y∣Ai] P(Ai),\begin{aligned} \mathbb{E}[Y]=\sum_i\mathbb{E}[Y\mid A_i]\,\mathbb{P}(A_{i}), \end{aligned}E[Y]=i∑​E[Y∣Ai​]P(Ai​),​

where terms with P(Ai)=0\mathbb{P}(A_i)=0P(Ai​)=0 are omitted. The law of total probability (LOTP) is the special case Y=𝟙1BY=\indicator_BY=𝟙1B​ of this partition form of the law of total expectation; the random-variable form E[E[Y∣X]]=E[Y]\mathbb{E}[\mathbb{E}[Y\mid X]]=\mathbb{E}[Y]E[E[Y∣X]]=E[Y] is Adam’s Law.

Interpretation¶

The overall average is a weighted average of the averages within disjoint cases: split the sample space into cases, find the mean of YYY in each case, and weight each case mean by the probability of the case.

In the diagram, the two cases have probabilities 0.30.30.3 and 0.70.70.7, drawn as bar widths, and conditional means 101010 and 222, drawn as bar heights. Each term E[Y∣Ai]P(Ai)\mathbb{E}[Y\mid A_i]\mathbb{P}(A_i)E[Y∣Ai​]P(Ai​) is the area of a bar, 333 and 1.41.41.4. The dashed level 4.44.44.4 has the same total area.

The cases must be disjoint so that no outcome is counted twice, and must cover the sample space so that none is missed; that is what a partition guarantees. A case with P(Ai)=0\mathbb{P}(A_i)=0P(Ai​)=0 has no conditional mean, but it also contributes nothing, so it is left out.

The theorem is useful when the cases make YYY simple. For the number NNN of tosses up to and including the first head, with head probability ppp, condition on the first toss; after a tail the count starts afresh, so EN=p⋅1+(1−p)(1+EN)\mathbb{E}N=p\cdot1+(1-p)(1+\mathbb{E}N)EN=p⋅1+(1−p)(1+EN), giving EN=1/p\mathbb{E}N=1/pEN=1/p, the mean of the first success distribution. With Y=𝟙1BY=\indicator_BY=𝟙1B​ the theorem becomes the law of total probability, and with the cases {X=x}\{X=x\}{X=x} for a discrete XXX it becomes Adam’s law.

The weights are the case probabilities, not equal weights: the unweighted average (10+2)/2=6(10+2)/2=6(10+2)/2=6 is wrong.

Key Results¶

  • For a partition {Ai}\{A_i\}{Ai​}, EY=∑i ⁣:P(Ai)>0E[Y∣Ai]P(Ai)\displaystyle \mathbb{E}Y=\sum_{i\colon\mathbb{P}(A_i)>0}\mathbb{E}[Y\mid A_i]\mathbb{P}(A_i)EY=i:P(Ai​)>0∑​E[Y∣Ai​]P(Ai​).
  • Putting Y=𝟙1BY=\indicator_BY=𝟙1B​ gives the law of total probability.
