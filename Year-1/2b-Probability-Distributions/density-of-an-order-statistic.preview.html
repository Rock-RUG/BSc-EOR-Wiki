Density of an Order Statistic¶

This page keeps the Probability Distributions density formula for order statistics. The basic definition of order statistics remains in Probability Theory for EOR.

Theorem¶

Let X1,…,XnX_1,\ldots,X_nX1​,…,Xn​ be iid continuous random variables with CDF FFF and PDF fff. For the kkkth order statistic X(k)X_{(k)}X(k)​, the density is

fX(k)(x)=n(n−1k−1)f(x)F(x)k−1(1−F(x))n−k.f_{X_{(k)}}(x) = n\binom{n-1}{k-1} f(x)F(x)^{k-1}\bigl(1-F(x)\bigr)^{n-k}.fX(k)​​(x)=n(k−1n−1​)f(x)F(x)k−1(1−F(x))n−k.

Interpretation¶

The formula counts how one observation lands near the chosen value, while the right number of observations fall below and above it.

Key Results¶

  • For k=1k=1k=1, the formula gives the density of the sample minimum.
  • For k=nk=nk=n, the formula gives the density of the sample maximum.
  • The binomial coefficient counts which observations fall below the order statistic.

Examples¶

  • For iid uniform random variables on [0,1][0,1][0,1], fX(k)(x)=n(n−1k−1)xk−1(1−x)n−kf_{X_{(k)}}(x)=n\binom{n-1}{k-1}x^{k-1}(1-x)^{n-k}fX(k)​​(x)=n(k−1n−1​)xk−1(1−x)n−k.
  • For the sample maximum X(n)X_{(n)}X(n)​ from iid continuous variables, the density is nf(x)F(x)n−1n f(x)F(x)^{n-1}nf(x)F(x)n−1.
