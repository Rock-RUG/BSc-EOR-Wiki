Cauchy-Schwarz Inequality¶

This page presents the Probability Distributions treatment. See also the treatment in Math II: Linear Algebra. Mastery is recorded separately for each course context.

Definition¶

Cauchy-Schwarz: if E[X2]\mathbb E[X^2]E[X2] and E[Y2]\mathbb E[Y^2]E[Y2] are finite, then

(E[XY])2≤E[X2]⋅E[Y2].\begin{align*} (\E{X Y})^2\leq \E{X^2}\cdot \E{Y^2}. \end{align*}(E[XY])2≤E[X2]⋅E[Y2].​
