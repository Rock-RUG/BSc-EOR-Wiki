Adam's Law with Extra Conditioning¶

Definition¶

Adam's Law with extra conditioning: for rvs X,Y,Z,X,Y,Z,X,Y,Z,

E[E[Y∣X,Z]∣Z]=E[Y∣Z].\begin{align*} \E{\E{Y\given X,Z}\given Z}=\E{Y\given Z}. \end{align*}E[E[Y∣X,Z]∣Z]=E[Y∣Z].​

This is Adam's Law with ZZZ kept as background information. It implies LOTP with extra conditioning by taking Y=1AY=\mathbf{1}_AY=1A​ and letting XXX encode the partition {Bi}\{B_i\}{Bi​}. Equivalently, once we condition on CCC, we can treat CCC as the new background information and apply the ordinary LOTP inside that conditional world.
