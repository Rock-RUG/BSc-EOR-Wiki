Location and Scale Property of the MGF¶

Proposition¶

For real constants aaa and bbb (no positivity is needed for bbb),

Ma+bX(t)=eatMX(bt)\begin{aligned} M_{a+bX}(t)=\mathrm{e}^{a t}M_X(b t) \end{aligned}Ma+bX​(t)=eatMX​(bt)​

Interpretation¶
