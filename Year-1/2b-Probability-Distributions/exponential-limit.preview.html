Exponential Limit¶

Theorem¶

For every real xxx,

lim⁡n→∞(1+xn)n=ex.\lim_{n\to\infty}\left(1+\frac{x}{n}\right)^n = \mathrm{e}^x.n→∞lim​(1+nx​)n=ex.

Interpretation¶

Many small multiplicative changes combine into an exponential. This limit also explains how rare-event binomial probabilities approach Poisson probabilities.
