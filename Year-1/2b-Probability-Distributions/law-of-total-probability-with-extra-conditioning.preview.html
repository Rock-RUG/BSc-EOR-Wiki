Law of Total Probability with Extra Conditioning¶

Definition¶

LOTP with extra conditioning: for P(()C)>0\P(C)>0P(()C)>0 and a partition {Bi}\{B_i\}{Bi​} with the conditional probabilities well-defined under CCC,

P(A∣C)=∑iP(A∣Bi,C) P(Bi∣C).\begin{align*} \P{A\given C}=\sum_i\P{A\given B_i,C}\,\P{B_i\given C}. \end{align*}P(A∣C)=i∑​P(A∣Bi​,C)P(Bi​∣C).​

Analogous versions hold for expectations, PMFs, and PDFs.
