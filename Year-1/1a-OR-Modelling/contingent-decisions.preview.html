Contingent Decisions¶

Definition¶

Contingent decisions are binary choices whose admissibility depends on other choices. If decision 2 is allowed only when decision 1 is yes, the implication is represented by

y2≤y1,y1,y2∈{0,1}.y_2\le y_1,\qquad y_1,y_2\in\{0,1\}.y2​≤y1​,y1​,y2​∈{0,1}.

Interpretation¶

The inequality prohibits precisely the combination “decision 2 yes, decision 1 no.” It does not force decision 2 to be selected when decision 1 is selected.
