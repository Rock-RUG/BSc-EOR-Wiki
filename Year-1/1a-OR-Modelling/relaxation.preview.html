Relaxation¶

Definition¶

A relaxation retains a problem's objective and removes or weakens constraints to obtain an easier problem. If FFF and FRF_RFR​ are the original and relaxed feasible sets, then F⊆FRF\subseteq F_RF⊆FR​.

Interpretation¶

The larger feasible set gives a benchmark that may be unattainable in the original problem. Its optimum helps assess a feasible solution obtained by another method.

Key Results¶

  • For minimization, the relaxed optimum is no greater than the original optimum; for maximization, it is no smaller.
  • A relaxed optimum that is also feasible for the original problem is optimal for the original problem.
  • An infeasible relaxed optimizer need not have a strictly better value; equality can occur.
