Entering Variable Rule¶

Definition¶

In the course's maximization simplex tableau, the entering variable rule selects a nonbasic variable with the most negative coefficient in the objective row. If several coefficients tie for most negative, any tied variable may be chosen.

Interpretation¶

Because the objective row is written with the objective on the left, a more negative coefficient corresponds to a higher improvement rate per unit increase of that variable.

Key Results¶

  • The selected variable defines the pivot column.
  • The greatest improvement rate is not necessarily the greatest total improvement, because allowable step sizes differ.
  • The ratio test determines how far the entering variable can increase while preserving feasibility.
