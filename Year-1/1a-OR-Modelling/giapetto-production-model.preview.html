Giapetto Production Model¶

Definition¶

The Giapetto production model chooses wooden soldier production xsx_sxs​ and train production xtx_txt​ to maximize profit. With the data in the course, the LP is

max⁡z=3xs+2xtsubject to2xs+xt≤100,xs+xt≤80,xs≤40,xs,xt≥0.\begin{aligned} \max\quad &z=3x_s+2x_t\\ \text{subject to}\quad &2x_s+x_t\le100,\\ &x_s+x_t\le80,\\ &x_s\le40,\\ &x_s,x_t\ge0. \end{aligned}maxsubject to​z=3xs​+2xt​2xs​+xt​≤100,xs​+xt​≤80,xs​≤40,xs​,xt​≥0.​

Interpretation¶

The example shows how revenues, material costs, labour requirements, and sales limits become a complete production-planning LP.

Key Results¶

  • The profit contribution is 3 per soldier and 2 per train.
  • Painting and carpentry constrain the two products jointly.
  • The soldier demand limit constrains only xsx_sxs​.

Examples¶

  • Producing 20 soldiers and 60 trains uses 100 painting hours and 80 carpentry hours.
  • Producing 40 soldiers and 40 trains violates the painting limit because it needs 120 hours.
