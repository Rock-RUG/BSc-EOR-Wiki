Continuous Newsvendor Rule¶

Proposition¶

For continuous demand with finite mean and CDF FFF, the newsvendor expected cost satisfies

G′(y)=(b+h)F(y)−b.G'(y)=(b+h)F(y)-b.G′(y)=(b+h)F(y)−b.

If FFF is continuous and strictly increasing through β=b/(b+h)\beta=b/(b+h)β=b/(b+h) and that quantile lies in the allowed quantity domain, the unique optimum is

y∗=F−1(β).y^*=F^{-1}(\beta).y∗=F−1(β).

Interpretation¶

The optimal stock is a quantile of demand, determined by the cost balance. Continuity alone does not guarantee uniqueness if the CDF has a flat segment at the critical probability.

Key Results¶

  • For uniform demand on [a,u][a,u][a,u], the optimum is a+(u−a)βa+(u-a)\betaa+(u−a)β.
  • For the course normal approximation, it is μ+σΦ−1(β)\mu+\sigma\Phi^{-1}(\beta)μ+σΦ−1(β).
  • A continuous demand approximation may give a noninteger answer even when actual items are indivisible.

Examples¶

  • Meal demand uniform on [80,120][80,120][80,120] with β=0.6\beta=0.6β=0.6 gives 104 meals.
  • Normal meal demand with mean 100 and standard deviation 20 gives about 105.07.
