Ambulance Location Model¶

Definition¶

The ambulance location model selects a minimum number of station towns while ensuring that every town is within 18 minutes of a selected station. Let yj=1y_j=1yj​=1 if a station is placed at town jjj, and let Ni={j:tij≤18}N_i=\{j:t_{ij}\le18\}Ni​={j:tij​≤18}. Then

Interpretation¶

Each coverage constraint requires at least one sufficiently close station. A station may cover several towns and a town may be covered by several stations.

Key Results¶

  • Include the town itself because its travel time to itself is zero.
  • The threshold includes exactly 18 minutes.
  • This is the set-covering structure illustrated by the exercise; no station-capacity limit is specified.
