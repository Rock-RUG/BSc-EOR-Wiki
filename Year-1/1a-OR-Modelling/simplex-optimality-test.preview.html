Simplex Optimality Test¶

Proposition¶

For a maximization LP with a feasible canonical tableau written as z+∑j∈Nqjxj=z0z+\sum_{j\in N}q_jx_j=z_0z+∑j∈N​qj​xj​=z0​, if qj≥0q_j\ge0qj​≥0 for every nonbasic variable, the current basic feasible solution is optimal. Here NNN is the nonbasic index set and every variable is nonnegative.

Interpretation¶

Every allowed increase of a nonbasic variable then leaves the objective unchanged or reduces it. The tableau therefore certifies that the current value cannot be exceeded.

Key Results¶

  • The sign test depends on the course objective-row convention.
  • Feasibility and canonical form are required before applying the test.
  • A negative coefficient calls for further simplex analysis; degeneracy means it does not alone prove that a strictly better feasible vector exists.
