Newsvendor Price Sensitivity¶

Proposition¶

The endpoints include ties with neighbouring quantities.

Interpretation¶

A higher selling price raises the opportunity cost of unmet demand. The interval identifies how far the price can move before the chosen stock level stops being optimal.

Key Results¶

  • The condition is for being an optimum, not necessarily the smallest optimum.

  • This analysis holds the demand distribution fixed, as the exercise does.
