Minimum-Ratio Test¶

Definition¶

For a feasible canonical tableau and an entering variable with constraint-column coefficients aika_{ik}aik​ and right-hand sides bi≥0b_i\ge0bi​≥0, the minimum-ratio test selects

θ=min⁡i:aik>0biaik.\theta=\min_{i:a_{ik}>0}\frac{b_i}{a_{ik}}.θ=i:aik​>0min​aik​bi​​.

A row attaining the minimum supplies the leaving basic variable. Zero ratios are eligible; only the denominator must be strictly positive.

Interpretation¶

The test finds the first basic variable that reaches zero as the entering variable increases. A larger step would violate nonnegativity.

Key Results¶

  • Rows with zero or negative pivot-column coefficients do not restrict the increase.
  • A tie may be broken arbitrarily in the course procedure and can lead to degeneracy.
  • If there is no positive coefficient and the objective improves along this column, the objective is unbounded.

  • The next ratios are 5/(1/2)=105/(1/2)=105/(1/2)=10 and 10/(5/2)=410/(5/2)=410/(5/2)=4, so s2s_2s2​ leaves.
