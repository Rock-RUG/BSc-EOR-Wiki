Probability Density Function¶

Definition¶

A probability density function of an absolutely continuous XXX is a nonnegative integrable function satisfying

P(X∈A)=∫AfX(x) dx,∫RfX(x) dx=1.\begin{gathered} \mathbb{P}(X\in A)=\int_Af_X(x)\,\mathrm{d}x,\\ \int_{\mathbb{R}}f_X(x)\,\mathrm{d}x=1. \end{gathered}P(X∈A)=∫A​fX​(x)dx,∫R​fX​(x)dx=1.​

Densities that agree except possibly on a set of zero length, for example at finitely many points, describe the same distribution.

Interpretation¶

A density describes how probability is spread over the real line. Areas under it are probabilities; the height at a point is not a probability.
