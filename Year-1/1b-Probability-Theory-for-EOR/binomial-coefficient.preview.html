Binomial Coefficient¶

Definition¶

Let n,k∈Nn,k\in\mathbb Nn,k∈N with 0≤k≤n0\le k\le n0≤k≤n. The binomial coefficient (nk)\binom nk(kn​) is the number of ways to form a combination of kkk objects from nnn distinct objects without regard to order, and is given by

(nk)=n!k!(n−k)!.\binom nk=\frac{n!}{k!(n-k)!}.(kn​)=k!(n−k)!n!​.

Interpretation¶

A binomial coefficient counts selections where order does not matter. It appears whenever a problem asks how many groups of a fixed size can be chosen.

Examples¶

  • From 101010 applicants, the number of possible interview groups of size 333 is (103)\binom{10}{3}(310​).
