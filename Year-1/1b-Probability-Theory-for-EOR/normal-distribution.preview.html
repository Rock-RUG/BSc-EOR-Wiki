Interpretation¶

A normal distribution is the familiar bell-shaped distribution. It is widely used because many sums and averages behave approximately normally.

Key Results¶

  • A normal distribution is determined by its mean and variance.
  • Standardizing a normal random variable gives a standard normal random variable.
  • Sums of independent normal random variables are normal.

Examples¶

  • Measurement error is often approximated by a normal distribution.
  • The average of many small independent effects is often modeled as normal.
  • Manufacturing deviations from a target dimension are frequently modeled with a normal distribution.
