Expectation¶

Definition¶

Interpretation¶

Expectation describes the center of a distribution by weighting each outcome by its probability. Sums and integrals express the same idea for discrete variables and variables with densities.

For the illustrated masses, the mean is E[X]=1(0.1)+2(0.2)+3(0.3)+4(0.4)=3\mathbb{E}[X]=1(0.1)+2(0.2)+3(0.3)+4(0.4)=3E[X]=1(0.1)+2(0.2)+3(0.3)+4(0.4)=3. This probability-weighted average is the balance point of the masses and the long-run average of iid draws.
