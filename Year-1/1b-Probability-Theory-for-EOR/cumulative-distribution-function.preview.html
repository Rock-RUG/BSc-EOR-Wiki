Cumulative Distribution Function¶

Definition¶

For a real-valued random variable XXX, its cumulative distribution function is

FX(x)=P(X≤x),x∈R.F_X(x)=\mathbb{P}(X\le x),\qquad x\in\mathbb R.FX​(x)=P(X≤x),x∈R.

It is nondecreasing and right-continuous, with lim⁡x→−∞FX(x)=0\lim_{x\to-\infty}F_X(x)=0limx→−∞​FX​(x)=0 and lim⁡x→∞FX(x)=1\lim_{x\to\infty}F_X(x)=1limx→∞​FX​(x)=1. These limits describe the notation FX(−∞)=0F_X(-\infty)=0FX​(−∞)=0 and FX(∞)=1F_X(\infty)=1FX​(∞)=1.

Interpretation¶

The CDF gives the probability of being at or below a threshold. It works for discrete, continuous, and mixed distributions.
