Binomial Distribution¶

Definition¶

Let nnn be a nonnegative integer, 0≤p≤10\le p\le10≤p≤1 and q=1−pq=1-pq=1−p. A discrete random variable XXX has a binomial distribution with parameters nnn and ppp, written X∼Bin⁡(n,p)X\sim\operatorname{Bin}(n,p)X∼Bin(n,p), if

P(X=k)=(nk)pkqn−k,k=0,1,…,n,\begin{gathered} \mathbb{P}(X=k)=\binom{n}{k}p^kq^{n-k},\\ k=0,1,\ldots,n, \end{gathered}P(X=k)=(kn​)pkqn−k,k=0,1,…,n,​

and the PMF is zero elsewhere. Equivalently, XXX is the sum of nnn iid Bernoulli variables with success probability ppp.

Interpretation¶

A binomial variable counts successes in a fixed number of independent trials with the same success probability.
