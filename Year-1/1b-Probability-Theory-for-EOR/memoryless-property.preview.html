Memoryless Property¶

Definition¶

A nonnegative waiting time is memoryless if, once it has lasted a certain time, the time still to wait has the same distribution as the original waiting time. For a nonnegative integer-valued GGG this means that for all nonnegative integers m,nm,nm,n

P(G≥m+n∣G≥m)=P(G≥n).\mathbb{P}(G\ge m+n\mid G\ge m)=\mathbb{P}(G\ge n).P(G≥m+n∣G≥m)=P(G≥n).

For an absolutely continuous nonnegative TTT it means that for all s,t≥0s,t\ge0s,t≥0

P(T>s+t∣T>s)=P(T>t).\mathbb{P}(T>s+t\mid T>s)=\mathbb{P}(T>t).P(T>s+t∣T>s)=P(T>t).

The conditioning events must have positive probability. The property also works in reverse. Suppose the waiting time is finite with probability 111 and all its tail probabilities are positive. Then a memoryless GGG has a Geo⁡(p)\operatorname{Geo}(p)Geo(p) distribution for some 0<p<10<p<10<p<1 (counting the failures before the first success), and a memoryless TTT has an Exp⁡(λ)\operatorname{Exp}(\lambda)Exp(λ) distribution for some λ>0\lambda>0λ>0. For the first-success distribution, which counts trials and takes the values 1,2,…1,2,\dots1,2,…, the same condition is written with strict tails: P(X>m+n∣X>m)=P(X>n)\mathbb{P}(X>m+n\mid X>m)=\mathbb{P}(X>n)P(X>m+n∣X>m)=P(X>n) for all nonnegative integers m,nm,nm,n.

Interpretation¶

For a memoryless waiting time, surviving the elapsed waiting period leaves the same distribution for the remaining waiting time.

Thus surviving until sss leaves the same distribution for the remaining wait. The continuous and discrete characterisations, including their assumptions, are stated in the definition above.
