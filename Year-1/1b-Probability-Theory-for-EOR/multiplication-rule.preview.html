Multiplication Rule for Counting¶

Definition¶

Consider a compound experiment made of two stages. If the first stage has aaa possible outcomes and, for each of them, the second stage has bbb possible outcomes, then the compound experiment has

a⋅ba\cdot ba⋅b

possible outcomes. This is the multiplication rule for counting. For kkk successive stages with n1,n2,…,nkn_1,n_2,\ldots,n_kn1​,n2​,…,nk​ choices it gives n1n2⋯nkn_1n_2\cdots n_kn1​n2​⋯nk​ ordered outcomes; in set notation, ∣A×B∣=∣A∣ ∣B∣|A\times B|=|A|\,|B|∣A×B∣=∣A∣∣B∣.

Interpretation¶

The multiplication rule counts ordered outcomes stage by stage: in a tree diagram, the number of leaves is the product of the numbers of branches at each level. The same tree carries probabilities, and multiplying along a path gives the probability of that path.

Counting the ordered outcomes gives 52⋅51=265252\cdot51=265252⋅51=2652 ordered draws, 4⋅3=124\cdot3=124⋅3=12 of them two aces, so 122652=1221\tfrac{12}{2652}=\tfrac1{221}265212​=2211​. Probabilities multiply along a path in the same way: P(A∩B)=P(A) P(B∣A)\mathbb{P}(A\cap B)=\mathbb{P}(A)\,\mathbb{P}(B\mid A)P(A∩B)=P(A)P(B∣A).

Key Results¶

  • The number of choices at a stage must not depend on the outcomes of earlier stages, although the choices themselves may.
  • Probability version: for events AAA and BBB with P(A)>0\mathbb{P}(A)>0P(A)>0, P(A∩B)=P(A)P(B∣A)\mathbb{P}(A\cap B)=\mathbb{P}(A)\mathbb{P}(B\mid A)P(A∩B)=P(A)P(B∣A); for several events the rule becomes a product of successive conditional probabilities.
  • For independent events, the conditional factor reduces to the marginal probability.

Examples¶

  • A code of two letters followed by three digits can be formed in 262⋅103=676 00026^2\cdot10^3=676\,000262⋅103=676000 ways.
  • Two dice give 6⋅6=366\cdot6=366⋅6=36 ordered outcomes, and nnn distinct objects can be ordered in n(n−1)⋯1=n!n(n-1)\cdots1=n!n(n−1)⋯1=n! ways.
  • Two cards drawn without replacement are both aces with probability 452⋅351=1221\frac4{52}\cdot\frac3{51}=\frac1{221}524​⋅513​=2211​: the chance of the first ace times the conditional chance of the second. In counts, 4⋅3=124\cdot3=124⋅3=12 of the 52⋅51=265252\cdot51=265252⋅51=2652 ordered draws.
