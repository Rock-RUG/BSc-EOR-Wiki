Sample Standardized Moment¶

Definition¶

Let x1,…,xnx_1,\ldots,x_nx1​,…,xn​ be observations with sample central moments mrm_rmr​ and sample standard deviation s>0s>0s>0. The rrrth sample standardized moment is

mrsr.\frac{m_r}{s^r}.srmr​​.

Interpretation¶

A sample standardized moment describes shape after removing scale. It allows features such as asymmetry and tail weight to be compared across data sets.

Key Results¶

  • Sample standardized moments remove the effect of scale.
  • The third sample standardized moment is sample skewness in common usage.
  • The fourth sample standardized moment is used to describe sample kurtosis.

Examples¶

  • Two data sets measured in different units can have comparable standardized moments.
  • A positive third standardized moment indicates right-skewed sample behavior.
  • A large fourth standardized moment suggests unusually heavy tails in the sample.
