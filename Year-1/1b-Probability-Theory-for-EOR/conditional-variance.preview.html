Conditional Variance¶

This page presents the Probability Theory for EOR treatment. See also the treatment in Probability Distributions. Mastery is recorded separately for each course context.

Definition¶

Let XXX be a random variable and let BBB be an event with P(B)>0P(B)>0P(B)>0. The conditional variance of XXX given BBB is

Var⁡(X∣B)=E[(X−E[X∣B])2∣B],\operatorname{Var}(X\mid B)=E[(X-E[X\mid B])^2\mid B],Var(X∣B)=E[(X−E[X∣B])2∣B],

provided the conditional expectations exist.

Interpretation¶

Conditional variance measures remaining uncertainty after some information is known. It describes how spread out the values are within the restricted situation.

Key Results¶

  • Conditional variance measures spread after conditioning on information.
  • The law of total variance decomposes total variability into within-condition and between-condition parts.
  • Conditional variance is zero when the condition determines the random variable exactly.

Examples¶

  • Delivery time may have smaller variance after conditioning on the shipping method.
  • Exam score variability may differ between students who studied and students who did not.
  • In a mixture distribution, each component can have its own conditional variance.
