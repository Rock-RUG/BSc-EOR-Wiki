Interpretation¶

Nonnegative variables can be either interior or stuck at the boundary. The Kuhn-Tucker conditions encode that boundary logic together with ordinary inequality slackness.

Key Results¶

  • If xh∗>0x_h^\ast>0xh∗​>0, the derivative condition for that variable becomes equality.
  • If a multiplier is positive, the corresponding ordinary inequality constraint is binding.
  • The conditions are necessary at regular local maxima.
