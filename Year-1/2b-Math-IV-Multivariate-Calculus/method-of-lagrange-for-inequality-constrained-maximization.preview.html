Method of Lagrange for Inequality-constrained Maximization¶

Theorem¶

Let A⊆RnA\subseteq\mathbb R^nA⊆Rn be open, and let f,g1,…,gk ⁣:A→Rf,g_1,\ldots,g_k\colon A\to\mathbb Rf,g1​,…,gk​:A→R be continuously differentiable. Consider

Interpretation¶

Inequality constraints are directional, so multiplier signs and slackness matter. A nonbinding constraint has slack and therefore contributes no multiplier force at a regular candidate.

Key Results¶

  • A binding constraint satisfies gi(x∗)=big_i(x^\ast)=b_igi​(x∗)=bi​.
  • A nonbinding constraint has zero multiplier by complementary slackness.
  • The theorem gives necessary, not sufficient, conditions.

Examples¶

  • For max⁡xy\max xymaxxy subject to x+y≤10x+y\le10x+y≤10, the Lagrangian is xy+λ(10−x−y)xy+\lambda(10-x-y)xy+λ(10−x−y).
  • If no inequality is binding at a local maximum, all inequality multipliers are zero and the unconstrained stationarity condition returns.
