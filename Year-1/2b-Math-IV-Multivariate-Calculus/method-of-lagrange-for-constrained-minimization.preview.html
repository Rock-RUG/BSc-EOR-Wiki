Method of Lagrange for Constrained Minimization¶

Theorem¶

Let A⊆RnA\subseteq\mathbb R^nA⊆Rn be open, and let the objective fff and all constraint functions gi,hjg_i,h_jgi​,hj​ be continuously differentiable on AAA. For

min⁡x∈Af(x)subject togi(x)≥bi,hj(x)=cj,\min_{x\in A} f(x)\quad\text{subject to}\quad g_i(x)\ge b_i,\quad h_j(x)=c_j,x∈Amin​f(x)subject togi​(x)≥bi​,hj​(x)=cj​,

Interpretation¶

For minimization, the inequality is written so the same nonnegative multiplier convention can be used. The resulting conditions are still only a candidate filter.

Key Results¶

  • The sign of the inequality term in the Lagrangian depends on the feasible side of the constraint.
  • Nonbinding inequalities have zero multipliers.
  • Equality multipliers remain unrestricted.

Examples¶

  • For min⁡ex−y\min e^{x-y}minex−y subject to x2+y2≥4x^2+y^2\ge4x2+y2≥4, use L=ex−y+λ(4−x2−y2)L=e^{x-y}+\lambda(4-x^2-y^2)L=ex−y+λ(4−x2−y2).
  • If x+y>1x+y>1x+y>1 at a local minimizer subject to x+y≥1x+y\ge1x+y≥1, complementary slackness forces the multiplier on that constraint to be zero.
