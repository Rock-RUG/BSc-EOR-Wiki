General Method of Lagrange¶

Theorem¶

Let A⊆RnA\subseteq\mathbb R^nA⊆Rn be open, and let f,gi,hjf,g_i,h_jf,gi​,hj​ be continuously differentiable on AAA. Interpretation¶

This method avoids a separate regularity check by adding a multiplier on the objective itself. The extra case captures points where the active constraints are degenerate.

Key Results¶

  • If λ0=1\lambda_0=1λ0​=1, the regular first-order necessary conditions are recovered.
  • If λ0=0\lambda_0=0λ0​=0, the condition signals linear dependence among active constraint gradients.
  • The method keeps nonregular feasible points inside the theorem.

Examples¶

  • For a regular optimum, choosing λ0=1\lambda_0=1λ0​=1 gives the usual Lagrangian conditions.
  • If active constraint gradients are linearly dependent, a solution may appear with λ0=0\lambda_0=0λ0​=0 and nonzero constraint multipliers.
