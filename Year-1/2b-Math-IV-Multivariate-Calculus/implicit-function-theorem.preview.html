Interpretation¶

The theorem says that a regular system of equations can locally be solved for the variables that must adjust. It also gives the local response of those adjusting variables when the chosen variables change.

Key Results¶

  • The determinant condition says the endogenous block is locally pinned down by the equations.
  • The theorem gives local existence, differentiability, and a derivative formula.
  • The one-equation case is the familiar slope formula for an implicit curve.
