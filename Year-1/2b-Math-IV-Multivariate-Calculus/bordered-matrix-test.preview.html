Bordered Matrix Test¶

Theorem¶

Let Q(x)=x⊤AxQ(x)=x^\top AxQ(x)=x⊤Ax with A∈Rn×nA\in\mathbb{R}^{n\times n}A∈Rn×n symmetric, and let the independent linear constraint set be Bx=0Bx=0Bx=0 with B∈Rm×nB\in\mathbb{R}^{m\times n}B∈Rm×n and rank⁡(B)=m\operatorname{rank}(B)=mrank(B)=m. The bordered matrix is

H=(0mBB⊤A).H= \begin{pmatrix} 0_m & B\\ B^\top & A \end{pmatrix}.H=(0m​B⊤​BA​).

Ignore the first 2m2m2m leading principal minors. For k=2m+1,…,m+nk=2m+1,\ldots,m+nk=2m+1,…,m+n:

  • If (−1)mdet⁡(Hk)>0(-1)^m\det(H_k)>0(−1)mdet(Hk​)>0 for all relevant kkk, then QQQ is positive definite on the constraint set.
  • If (−1)k−mdet⁡(Hk)>0(-1)^{k-m}\det(H_k)>0(−1)k−mdet(Hk​)>0 for all relevant kkk, then QQQ is negative definite on the constraint set.

Interpretation¶

The bordered matrix test checks curvature after linear constraints remove some directions. It is the constrained version of reading definiteness from determinant signs.

Key Results¶

  • Definiteness on the whole space can change after restricting to a constraint set.
  • For positive definiteness, all relevant signs match (−1)m(-1)^m(−1)m.
  • For negative definiteness, relevant signs alternate and the full determinant has sign (−1)n(-1)^n(−1)n.
