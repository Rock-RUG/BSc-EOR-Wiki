Ratio Test¶

Proposition¶

Consider the series ∑n=1∞an\displaystyle \sum_{n=1}^\infty a_nn=1∑∞​an​ where an∈R.a_n\in\mathbb{R}\text{.}an​∈R. Let L=lim⁡n→∞∣an+1an∣,\displaystyle L=\lim_{n \to \infty} \left| \dfrac{a_{n+1}}{a_n} \right|\text{,}L=n→∞lim​​an​an+1​​​, then:

  • If L<1,L < 1\text{,}L<1, the series converges. Moreover, ∑n=1∞∣an∣\displaystyle \sum_{n=1}^\infty |a_n|n=1∑∞​∣an​∣ also converges.

Interpretation¶

The ratio test states that a series converges if the limit of the ratio of consecutive terms is less than 1, and diverges if it is greater than 1.
