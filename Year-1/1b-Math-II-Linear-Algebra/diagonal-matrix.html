    Diagonal Matrix¶

    Definition¶

    A diagonal matrix is a square matrix whose off-diagonal entries are all zero.

    Interpretation¶

    A diagonal matrix only acts separately on each coordinate.

    Key Results¶

    • The determinant of a diagonal matrix is the product of its diagonal entries.
    • The product and sum of diagonal matrices of the same size are diagonal.

    Examples¶

    • (200−1)\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}(20​0−1​) is diagonal.
    • InI_nIn​ and 0n0_n0n​ are diagonal.
