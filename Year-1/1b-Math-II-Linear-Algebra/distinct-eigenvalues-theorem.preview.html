Distinct Eigenvalues Theorem¶

Theorem¶

If AAA is a linear operator with distinct eigenvalues λ1,…,λn\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_nλ1​,…,λn​ and corresponding eigenvectors v1,…,vnv_1,\ldots,v_nv1​,…,vn​, then (v1,…,vn)(v_1,\ldots,v_n)(v1​,…,vn​) is linearly independent.

Interpretation¶

Different eigenvalues produce genuinely different eigendirections.

Key Results¶

  • An n×nn\times nn×n matrix with nnn distinct eigenvalues is diagonalizable.
  • The theorem is a main reason eigenvectors can form useful bases.

Examples¶

  • A 2×22\times22×2 diagonal matrix with diagonal entries 222 and 333 has two independent eigenvectors.
  • A 3×33\times33×3 matrix with three distinct eigenvalues has three independent eigenvectors.
