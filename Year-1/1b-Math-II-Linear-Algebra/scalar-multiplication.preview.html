Scalar Multiplication¶

Definition¶

Scalar multiplication is the operation that takes a scalar α∈F\alpha\in\mathbb Fα∈F and a vector v∈Vv\in Vv∈V and returns a vector αv∈V\alpha v\in Vαv∈V.

Interpretation¶

Scalar multiplication stretches, shrinks, or reverses vectors according to the scalar.
