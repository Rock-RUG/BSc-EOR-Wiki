Direct Sum¶

Definition¶

Let V1,V2,…,VmV_1,V_2,\ldots,V_mV1​,V2​,…,Vm​ be subspaces of VVV, and let W=V1+V2+⋯+VmW=V_1+V_2+\cdots+V_mW=V1​+V2​+⋯+Vm​. If every w∈Ww\in Ww∈W can be written in exactly one way as

w=v1+v2+⋯+vm,w=v_1+v_2+\cdots+v_m,w=v1​+v2​+⋯+vm​,

with vi∈Viv_i\in V_ivi​∈Vi​, then WWW is the direct sum of the subspaces, denoted

W=V1⊕V2⊕⋯⊕Vm.W=V_1\oplus V_2\oplus\cdots\oplus V_m.W=V1​⊕V2​⊕⋯⊕Vm​.

Interpretation¶

A direct sum is a sum of subspaces without overlap that would create non-unique decompositions.

Key Results¶

  • For two subspaces, V1+V2V_1+V_2V1​+V2​ is direct exactly when V1∩V2={0}V_1\cap V_2=\{\boldsymbol 0\}V1​∩V2​={0}.
  • Many questions about the concept reduce to solving equations involving linear combinations.

Examples¶

  • In R2\mathbb R^2R2, vectors such as (1,0)(1,0)(1,0) and (0,1)(0,1)(0,1) illustrate the concept concretely.
