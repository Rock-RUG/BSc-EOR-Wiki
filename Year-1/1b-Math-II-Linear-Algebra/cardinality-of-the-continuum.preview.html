Cardinality of the Continuum¶

Definition¶

The cardinality of the continuum, often denoted c\mathfrak cc, is the cardinality of the real numbers:

c=card⁡(R).\mathfrak c=\operatorname{card}(\mathbb R).c=card(R).

Interpretation¶

The continuum is the size of the real line, and it is larger than countable infinity.
