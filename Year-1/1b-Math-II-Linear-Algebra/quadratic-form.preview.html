Quadratic Form¶

Definition¶

A quadratic form on Rn\mathbb R^nRn is a function Q ⁣:Rn→RQ\colon\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb RQ:Rn→R of the form

Q(v)=vTAv=∑i=1n∑j=1naijvivj,Q(v)=v^TAv=\sum_{i=1}^n\sum_{j=1}^n a_{ij}v_iv_j,Q(v)=vTAv=i=1∑n​j=1∑n​aij​vi​vj​,

where A=(aij)A=(a_{ij})A=(aij​) is a real n×nn\times nn×n matrix and vvv a column vector. The matrix can always be chosen symmetric, and the symmetric choice is unique: it is called the matrix of QQQ.

Interpretation¶

A quadratic form turns a vector into a second-degree expression determined by a symmetric matrix.
