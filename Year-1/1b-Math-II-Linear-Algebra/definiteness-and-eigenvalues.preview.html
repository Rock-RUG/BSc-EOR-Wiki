Definiteness and Eigenvalues¶

Theorem¶

For a real symmetric matrix AAA, definiteness is completely determined by the signs of the eigenvalues: positive definite means all eigenvalues are positive, positive semidefinite means all are nonnegative, negative definite means all are negative, negative semidefinite means all are nonpositive, and indefinite means both positive and negative eigenvalues occur.

Interpretation¶

Eigenvalues reveal the sign behavior of a symmetric matrix's quadratic form.

Key Results¶

  • This theorem converts a quadratic-form problem into an eigenvalue problem.
  • It applies to real symmetric matrices.

Examples¶

  • A diagonal matrix with diagonal entries 222 and 333 is positive definite.
  • A diagonal matrix with diagonal entries 222 and −3-3−3 is indefinite.
