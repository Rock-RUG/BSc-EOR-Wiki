Triangle Inequality for Vector Norms¶

Theorem¶

For vectors x,y∈Rnx,y\in\mathbb R^nx,y∈Rn, the triangle inequality states ∥x+y∥≤∥x∥+∥y∥\|x+y\|\leq\|x\|+\|y\|∥x+y∥≤∥x∥+∥y∥.

Interpretation¶

The length of one side of a triangle is at most the sum of the other two sides.

Key Results¶

  • The triangle inequality follows from the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality.
  • It is one of the defining properties of a norm.

Examples¶

  • ∥(1,0)+(0,1)∥=2≤2\|(1,0)+(0,1)\|=\sqrt2\leq2∥(1,0)+(0,1)∥=2​≤2.
  • If xxx and yyy point in the same direction, equality can hold.
