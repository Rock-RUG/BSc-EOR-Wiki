Matrix Scalar Multiplication¶

Definition¶

The scalar multiple αA\alpha AαA of a matrix A=(aij)A=(a_{ij})A=(aij​) is the matrix whose entries are (αaij)(\alpha a_{ij})(αaij​).

Interpretation¶

Scalar multiplication stretches every entry of the matrix by the same factor.

Key Results¶

  • Scalar multiplication distributes over matrix addition.
  • 0A0A0A is the zero matrix and 1A=A1A=A1A=A.
