Euler's Identity¶

Corollary¶

Setting z=πz=\piz=π in Euler's formula gives eiπ+1=0.\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}\pi}+1=0.eiπ+1=0.

Interpretation¶

It is the most beautiful mathematical formula/equation.

Key Results¶

  • Euler's identity involves three basic arithmetic operations exactly once: addition, multiplication, and exponentiation.
  • It also links five fundamental mathematical constants: the additive identity 000, the multiplicative identity 111, the fundamental circle constant π\piπ, the base of the natural exponential e\mathrm ee, and the imaginary unit i\mathrm ii.

Examples¶

  • “If you don’t think this formula is beautiful, you have no soul as a mathematician.”
