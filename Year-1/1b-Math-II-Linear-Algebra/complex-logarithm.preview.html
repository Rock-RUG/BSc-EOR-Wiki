Complex Logarithm¶

Definition¶

A complex logarithm of a nonzero complex number zzz, defined to be any complex number www for which ew=ze^{w}=zew=z. Such a number www is denoted by log⁡z\log zlogz. If zzz is given in polar form as z=reiφz=re^{i\varphi}z=reiφ, where r>0r>0r>0 and φ\varphiφ are real numbers, then ln⁡r+iφ\ln r+\mathrm i \varphilnr+iφ is one logarithm of zzz, and all the complex logarithms of zzz are exactly the numbers of the form ln⁡r+i(φ+2πk)\ln r+\mathrm i\left(\varphi +2\pi k\right)lnr+i(φ+2πk) for integers k.k.k. Interpretation¶

The complex logarithm is a multivalued function since the complex exponential function is not bijective.
