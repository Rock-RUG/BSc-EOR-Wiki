Sarrus' Rule¶

Method¶

Sarrus’ rule is a mnemonic for computing the determinant of a 3×33\times33×3 matrix by adding the products along the falling diagonals and subtracting the products along the rising diagonals.

Interpretation¶

Sarrus’ rule is a quick visual method, but it only works for 3×33\times33×3 matrices.

Key Results¶

  • The three falling diagonal products receive positive signs.
  • The three rising diagonal products receive negative signs.

Examples¶

  • For I3I_3I3​, Sarrus’ rule gives 111.
  • For a matrix with two equal rows, Sarrus’ rule gives determinant 000.
