Polynomial Long Division¶

Definition¶

Interpretation¶

It is the polynomial analogue of integer division with quotient and remainder.

Key Results¶

  • The quotient and remainder are uniquely determined.
  • Polynomial division gives a quotient and remainder with the remainder of smaller degree.
