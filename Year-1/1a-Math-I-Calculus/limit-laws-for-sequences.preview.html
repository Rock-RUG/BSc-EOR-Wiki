Interpretation¶

Limit laws for sequences describe how convergence behaves under algebraic operations.

They allow limits of complicated sequences to be inferred from simpler ones.

Key Results¶

  • Limit laws apply only to convergent sequences.
  • Algebraic combinations preserve convergence.
  • Division requires non-zero limiting denominators.
