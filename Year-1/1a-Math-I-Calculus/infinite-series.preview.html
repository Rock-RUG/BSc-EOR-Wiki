Interpretation¶

An infinite series represents the accumulation of infinitely many terms.

Its behavior is determined by the convergence of its partial sums.

Key Results¶

  • A necessary condition for an infinite series ∑n=1∞an\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^\infty a_nn=1∑∞​an​ to converge is that its terms ana_nan​'s tend to zero.
  • A series may still diverge even if its terms tend to zero.
