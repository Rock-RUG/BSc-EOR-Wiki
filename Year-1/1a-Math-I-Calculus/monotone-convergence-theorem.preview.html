Interpretation¶

The monotone convergence theorem guarantees convergence based on order and boundedness alone.

It shows that monotonicity provides strong control over the long-term behaviour of sequences.

Key Results¶

  • Every bounded monotone sequence is convergent.
  • The limit is given by the supremum or infimum (given a decreasing sequence with lower bound) of the sequence.
