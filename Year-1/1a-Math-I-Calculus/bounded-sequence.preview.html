Boundedness is a common hypothesis in convergence results. Every convergent sequence is bounded, so an unbounded sequence diverges. The monotone convergence theorem adds monotonicity to obtain convergence. In the figure, the even-indexed terms form a convergent subsequence with limit 111.

Math III shows that every bounded sequence has a convergent subsequence: the Bolzano–Weierstrass theorem.

Key Results¶

  • Every convergent sequence is bounded.
  • A bounded sequence need not converge.
  • Every finite sequence is bounded.

Examples¶

  • an=1/na_n=1/nan​=1/n is bounded.
  • an=(−1)na_n=(-1)^nan​=(−1)n is bounded but not convergent.
