Finite Sequence¶

Definition¶

Let (ai)i=0n(a_i)_{i=0}^n(ai​)i=0n​ be a sequence. We say (ai)i=0n(a_i)_{i=0}^n(ai​)i=0n​ is a finite sequence if nnn is a natural number.

Interpretation¶

A finite sequence consists of a fixed number of terms indexed by natural numbers.

It serves as a bridge between ordered tuples and infinite sequences.

Key Results¶

  • Finite sequences have a last term.
  • Every finite sequence can be viewed as a function on a finite index set.
  • Finite sequences are always bounded.
