Interpretation¶

L’Hôpital’s rule provides a method to evaluate limits involving indeterminate forms by comparing the rates of change of the numerator and denominator.

It replaces a difficult limit of a quotient by a potentially simpler limit involving derivatives.

Key Results¶

  • L’Hôpital’s rule applies only to indeterminate forms of type 0/00/00/0 or ∞/∞.\infty/\infty\text{.}∞/∞.
  • Differentiability of both numerator and denominator is required in a punctured neighbourhood of the point.
  • The rule must not be used to justify limits that are needed to establish the derivatives involved.
  • The result is always a determinate extended real number.
