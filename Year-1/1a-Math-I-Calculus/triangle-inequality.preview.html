Triangle Inequality¶

Proposition¶

For all real numbers xxx and y,y\text{,}y,

∣x+y∣≤∣x∣+∣y∣.|x+y|\leq |x|+|y|.∣x+y∣≤∣x∣+∣y∣.

Interpretation¶

The triangle inequality bounds the size of a sum by the sizes of its components.

It formalises the idea that direct paths are shorter than indirect ones.

Key Results¶

  • Absolute values interact subadditively under addition.
  • The inequality provides fundamental bounds in analysis.
