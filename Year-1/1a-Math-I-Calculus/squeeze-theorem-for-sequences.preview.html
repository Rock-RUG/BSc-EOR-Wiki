Interpretation¶

The squeeze theorem for sequences establishes convergence using termwise inequalities.

It reduces convergence questions to bounding arguments.

Key Results¶

  • The bounded sequence must lie between two convergent sequences.
  • Equality of outer limits enforces convergence of the middle sequence.
  • The theorem does not require monotonicity.
