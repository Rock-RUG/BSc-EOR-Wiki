Asymptotic Equivalence¶

Definition¶

Interpretation¶

Asymptotic equivalence describes when two functions behave almost identically near a given point or at infinity, up to negligible relative error.

Rather than comparing absolute differences, this notion compares relative growth rates and is especially useful for simplifying limits.
