Continuity¶

Definition¶

Let f ⁣:X→Rf\colon X\to\mathbb{R}f:X→R and x0∈X.x_0\in X\text{.}x0​∈X. We say that fff is continuous at x0\boldsymbol{x_0}x0​ if for every ε>0\varepsilon>0ε>0 there exists δ>0\delta>0δ>0 such that, for every x∈X,x\in X\text{,}x∈X,

Interpretation¶

Continuity expresses the idea that small changes in the input of a function lead to small changes in its output.

A continuous function does not exhibit sudden jumps or breaks at the point under consideration.
