Interpretation¶

One-sided limits describe the behaviour of a function as the input approaches a point from one direction only.

They allow limits to be defined at boundary points and points of discontinuity.

Key Results¶

  • One-sided limits may exist independently of each other.
  • Equality of both one-sided limits implies existence of the two-sided limit.
  • One-sided limits are essential for defining one-sided continuity and derivatives.

Examples¶

  • The signum function has distinct one-sided limits at the origin.
