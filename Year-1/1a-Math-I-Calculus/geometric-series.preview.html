Interpretation¶

A geometric series models repeated proportional change, where each term is obtained by multiplying the previous one by a fixed ratio.

It is one of the few infinite series whose convergence behaviour and sum can be described explicitly.

Key Results¶

  • If a≠0,a\neq0\text{,}a=0, the infinite geometric series converges if and only if ∣q∣<1.|q|<1\text{.}∣q∣<1. The all-zero series is the only exception.
