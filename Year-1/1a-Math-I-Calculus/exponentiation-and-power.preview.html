Interpretation¶

For positive integer exponents, exponentiation describes repeated multiplication. Roots, reciprocals, and limits extend this idea to rational and real exponents.

The domain of a power matters: even roots require nonnegative inputs, odd roots allow negative inputs, and an irrational real exponent generally requires a positive base.
