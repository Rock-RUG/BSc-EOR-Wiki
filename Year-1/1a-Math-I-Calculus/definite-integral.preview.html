Definite Integral¶

Interpretation¶

The definite integral represents the accumulated value of a function over an interval.

It encodes net area and serves as a fundamental link between summation and continuous accumulation.

Key Results¶

  • Integrable functions admit additive behaviour over subintervals.
  • Definite integrals are invariant under partition refinement.
  • A definite integral measures signed area: regions above the xxx-axis contribute positively and regions below contribute negatively. When f≥0,f\geq0\text{,}f≥0, it agrees with the ordinary area under the graph.
