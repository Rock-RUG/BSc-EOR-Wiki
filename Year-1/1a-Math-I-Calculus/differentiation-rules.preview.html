Interpretation¶

Differentiation rules provide systematic methods for computing derivatives without returning to first principles.

They enable efficient differentiation of complex expressions built from simpler functions.

Key Results¶

  • Differentiation rules preserve differentiability under algebraic operations.
  • The product and quotient rules account for interactions between factors.
  • Constant multiples factor out of derivatives.

  • Together with the chain rule, the standard formulas give
