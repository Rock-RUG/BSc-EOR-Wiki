Interpretation¶

Trigonometric functions encode angular relationships using coordinates on the unit circle.

They provide periodic models for oscillatory behavior.

Key Results¶

  • Sine and cosine are defined for all real numbers.
  • Trigonometric functions are periodic.
  • Tangent is defined only where cosine is nonzero.
