Chain Rule¶

Proposition¶

Let X,X\text{,}X, YYY be subsets of R,\mathbb{R}\text{,}R, let x∈Xx \in Xx∈X and y∈Y,y \in Y\text{,}y∈Y, and let f ⁣:X→Yf \colon X \to Yf:X→Y be a function such that f(x)=y,f(x) =y\text{,}f(x)=y, and such that fff is differentiable at x.x\text{.}x. Interpretation¶

The chain rule describes how differentiation behaves under composition of functions.

It formalises the propagation of rates of change through intermediate variables.

Key Results¶

  • The composition of differentiable functions is differentiable.
  • The derivative of a composition is the product of derivatives evaluated at appropriate points.
