Interpretation¶

Divergence to infinity describes functions whose values grow beyond all bounds near a given point.

Key Results¶

  • Divergence to infinity is a form of non-finite limit.
  • Divergence may differ on one-sided neighbourhoods.
  • It can be defined similarly for a sequence diverging to infinity.
