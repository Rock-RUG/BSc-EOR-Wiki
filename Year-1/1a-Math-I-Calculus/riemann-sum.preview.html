Definition¶

Interpretation¶

Riemann sums approximate the accumulation of a function by finite weighted sums.

They provide the link between discrete approximations and continuous integration.

Examples¶

  • Left and right Riemann sums approximate areas differently.
  • Regular subdivisions lead to uniform rectangle widths.
