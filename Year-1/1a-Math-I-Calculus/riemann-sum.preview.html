Where fff is negative the terms are negative, so a Riemann sum approximates signed area: parts below the axis count negatively.

Riemann sums are the bridge between sums and integrals: the Riemann integral is defined as their limit, and conversely an integral can be estimated numerically by a Riemann sum with many pieces. A common slip on a regular subdivision of [a,b][a,b][a,b] into nnn pieces is to take the width as 1n\tfrac{1}{n}n1​.
