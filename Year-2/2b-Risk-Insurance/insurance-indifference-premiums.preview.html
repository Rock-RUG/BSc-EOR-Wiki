Insurance Indifference Premiums¶

Definition¶

For initial policyholder wealth www, strictly increasing utility uuu and full insurance of loss XXX, the maximum acceptable premium P+P^+P+ solves u(w−P+)=E[u(w−X)]u(w-P^+)=\mathbb E[u(w-X)]u(w−P+)=E[u(w−X)]. For insurer wealth WWW and utility UUU, its minimum acceptable premium P−P^-P− solves U(W)=E[U(W+P−−X)]U(W)=\mathbb E[U(W+P^--X)]U(W)=E[U(W+P−−X)], when finite solutions exist.

Interpretation¶

One equation compares insured and uninsured wealth for the customer; the other compares taking the contract with taking no risk for the insurer. A trade is acceptable to both when the premium lies between their thresholds.

Key Results¶

  • Under concavity and well-defined expectations, P+≥E[X]P^+\ge\mathbb E[X]P+≥E[X] and P−≥E[X]P^-\ge\mathbb E[X]P−≥E[X].
  • A mutually acceptable full-insurance premium exists if P−≤P+P^-\le P^+P−≤P+.
  • A risk-neutral insurer has P−=E[X]P^-=\mathbb E[X]P−=E[X] in this expense-free model.
  • Exponential utilities with different coefficients produce different thresholds; finiteness requires the respective exponential loss moments.
