Loss Elimination Ratio¶

Definition¶

For 0<E[X]<∞0<\mathbb E[X]<\infty0<E[X]<∞ and ordinary deductible d≥0d\ge0d≥0, the loss elimination ratio is

LER⁡(d)=E[X]−E[(X−d)+]E[X]=E[min⁡(X,d)]E[X].\operatorname{LER}(d)={\mathbb E[X]-\mathbb E[(X-d)_+]\over\mathbb E[X]}={\mathbb E[\min(X,d)]\over\mathbb E[X]}.LER(d)=E[X]E[X]−E[(X−d)+​]​=E[X]E[min(X,d)]​.

Interpretation¶

The ratio measures the share of expected ground-up loss removed from insurer payments. It concerns expected amounts, rather than the fraction of claims receiving no payment.
