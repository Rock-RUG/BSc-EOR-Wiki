Cumulant Generating Function¶

Definition¶

If the MGF MX(t)=E[etX]M_X(t)=\mathbb E[e^{tX}]MX​(t)=E[etX] is finite in a neighbourhood of zero, the cumulant generating function is KX(t)=log⁡MX(t)K_X(t)=\log M_X(t)KX​(t)=logMX​(t) on that domain. Its derivatives at zero are called cumulants.

Interpretation¶

Taking logarithms turns products of independent MGFs into sums. This makes cumulants a convenient way to describe aggregate risk.
