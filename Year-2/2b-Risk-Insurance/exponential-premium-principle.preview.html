Exponential Premium Principle¶

Definition¶

For risk-aversion parameter α>0\alpha>0α>0 and loss XXX with E[eαX]<∞\mathbb E[e^{\alpha X}]<\inftyE[eαX]<∞, the exponential premium is Πα(X)=α−1log⁡E[eαX]=KX(α)/α\Pi_\alpha(X)=\alpha^{-1}\log\mathbb E[e^{\alpha X}]=K_X(\alpha)/\alphaΠα​(X)=α−1logE[eαX]=KX​(α)/α.

Interpretation¶

This is the indifference price for exponential wealth utility. It gives extra weight to large losses and may be infinite for losses whose positive exponential moments do not exist.

Key Results¶

  • Jensen gives Πα(X)≥E[X]\Pi_\alpha(X)\ge\mathbb E[X]Πα​(X)≥E[X].
  • Translation consistency and independent additivity follow from exponential products.
  • Iterativity follows from the tower property: eαΠα(X∣Y)=E[eαX∣Y]e^{\alpha\Pi_\alpha(X\mid Y)}=\mathbb E[e^{\alpha X}\mid Y]eαΠα​(X∣Y)=E[eαX∣Y].
  • It respects exponential order and hence stop-loss order.
  • For bounded XXX, Πα(X)≤ess sup⁡X\Pi_\alpha(X)\le\operatorname{ess\,sup}XΠα​(X)≤esssupX.
  • Near α=0\alpha=0α=0 with sufficient moments and a finite MGF, Πα(X)=E[X]+αVar⁡(X)/2+o(α)\Pi_\alpha(X)=\mathbb E[X]+\alpha\operatorname{Var}(X)/2+o(\alpha)Πα​(X)=E[X]+αVar(X)/2+o(α).

Examples¶

For exponential severity of rate λ\lambdaλ and 0<α<λ0<\alpha<\lambda0<α<λ, premium is α−1log⁡[λ/(λ−α)]\alpha^{-1}\log[\lambda/(\lambda-\alpha)]α−1log[λ/(λ−α)]. At or above λ\lambdaλ it is infinite.
