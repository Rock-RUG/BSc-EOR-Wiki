Stationary Bonus-Malus Distribution and Premium¶

Definition¶

A stationary distribution at risk rate λ\lambdaλ is a row vector π(λ)\pi(\lambda)π(λ) satisfying πP=π\pi P=\piπP=π, πl≥0\pi_l\ge0πl​≥0 and ∑lπl=1\sum_l\pi_l=1∑l​πl​=1. With class premiums clc_lcl​, the stationary mean premium is b(λ)=∑lπl(λ)clb(\lambda)=\sum_l\pi_l(\lambda)c_lb(λ)=∑l​πl​(λ)cl​.

Interpretation¶

The stationary distribution leaves class proportions unchanged after another year. Convergence from arbitrary initial proportions is a separate property, not a consequence of solving the stationary equation alone.

Key Results¶

  • A finite irreducible chain has a unique stationary distribution; aperiodicity additionally guarantees convergence from every initial distribution.
  • π\piπ is a left eigenvector of PPP for eigenvalue one, normalised to sum to one.
  • For the three-state two-year scheme, π=(p,pq,q2)\pi=(p,pq,q^2)π=(p,pq,q2).
  • If the first two states cost ccc and the third costs aaa, b(λ)=c(1−e−2λ)+ae−2λb(\lambda)=c(1-e^{-2\lambda})+ae^{-2\lambda}b(λ)=c(1−e−2λ)+ae−2λ.
