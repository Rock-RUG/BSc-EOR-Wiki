Bonus-Malus Transition Matrix¶

Definition¶

At a fixed claim rate λ\lambdaλ, let pij(λ)=P(Lt+1=j∣Lt=i,λ)p_{ij}(\lambda)=\mathbb{P}(L_{t+1}=j\mid L_t=i,\lambda)pij​(λ)=P(Lt+1​=j∣Lt​=i,λ). The transition matrix P(λ)=(pij)\boldsymbol{P}(\lambda)=(p_{ij})P(λ)=(pij​) has nonnegative entries and each row sums to one. For row-vector level probabilities at\boldsymbol{a}_tat​, the next-year distribution is at+1=atP\boldsymbol{a}_{t+1}=\boldsymbol{a}_t\boldsymbol{P}at+1​=at​P.

Interpretation¶

The bonus-malus system supplies the rule assigning next year's class from the current state and claim count. The entries above are conditional probabilities given the current state; the matrix product adds their contributions to the next-year class probabilities.

Red arrows are years with a claim: from every state they lead to state 1, which fills the first column with ppp. Green arrows are claim-free years: state 1 moves to 2, while states 2 and 3 move to 3. The arrows leaving state iii give row iii, and their probabilities sum to p+q=1p+q=1p+q=1.

The state must store enough history for the transition rule. For a two-year claim-free discount, a simple high/low premium label may omit the information needed for next year.
