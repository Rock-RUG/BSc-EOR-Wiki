Grim Trigger Strategy¶

Definition¶

In an infinitely repeated game with discount factor δ∈(0,1)\delta\in(0,1)δ∈(0,1), fix a target action profile aca^cac and a stage-game Nash equilibrium a∗a^*a∗. The grim trigger strategy of player iii is

  • play aica_i^caic​ at t=0t=0t=0 and in every period ttt whose history hth^tht consists only of aca^cac;
  • play ai∗a_i^*ai∗​ after every other history, that is, forever once anyone has deviated from aca^cac.

It is the trigger strategy whose punishment phase never ends.

Interpretation¶

Among trigger strategies that punish with a given stage equilibrium a∗a^*a∗, grim trigger punishes longest, so it needs the least patience. Its incentive check is short. After any deviation, play is a∗a^*a∗ forever, which is a Nash equilibrium of every such subgame. Grim trigger is unforgiving: a single mistake destroys cooperation forever, even though both players would prefer to restart. This makes it fragile as a description of behaviour, but it is the standard device for showing which outcomes can be sustained at all.
