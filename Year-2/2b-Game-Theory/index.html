    Game Theory¶

    Year 2 · Block 2b · Economics and finance

    Not yet reviewed by the Game Theory lecturer. Details

    These pages are a provisional release: they have not yet been fully reviewed by the lecturer, so some pages may go beyond what the course covers and some notation may differ from the lectures. Treat them as a student-made study aid rather than as course material, and always follow the official course materials where they differ.

    Static games of complete information lead to mixed strategies, dynamic games and subgame perfection, repeated games, games of incomplete information, auctions and signalling.

    How to read this courseNotation, shared pages and conventions

    These notes introduce course-specific concepts. Probability and calculus already taught in Year 1 have one canonical page and are linked under Before you start rather than repeated. The course has one lecture in each of its seven teaching weeks, so each lecture number equals its week number; the weekly tutorial solutions belong to the same week. Concepts appear at their first introduction and are reused by links afterwards.
