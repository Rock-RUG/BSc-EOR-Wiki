Life Insurance Valuation Recursions¶

Proposition¶

For annual unit whole life insurance, conditioning on the first year gives the valuation recursion

Ax=vqx+vpxAx+1,A_x=vq_x+vp_xA_{x+1},Ax​=vqx​+vpx​Ax+1​,

where vvv is the annual discount factor and px=1−qxp_x=1-q_xpx​=1−qx​. The first term represents death during the year; the second represents survival followed by the remaining contract.

Interpretation¶

A long contract can be valued one year at a time. This also explains why product decompositions have both financial and survival discount factors.

Key Results¶

  • Let Ax:nA_{x:\angl{n}}Ax:n​​ denote annual-settlement unit nnn-year endowment APV. Then Ax:n=v[qx+pxAx+1:n-1]A_{x:\angl{n}}=v[q_x+p_xA_{x+1:\angl{n-1}}]Ax:n​​=v[qx​+px​Ax+1:n-1​​], with Ax:0=1A_{x:\angl{0}}=1Ax:0​​=1.
  • Annual term insurance uses the same recursion but a terminal value of zero.
  • Backward numerical valuation requires a terminal age or a justified tail treatment.
  • The recursion concerns conditional values at successively older ages, not independent new policies.
