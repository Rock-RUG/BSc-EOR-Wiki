Annuity Certain¶

Definition¶

An annuity certain makes specified payments independently of survival. For nnn annual unit payments, the annuity-due pays at 0,…,n−10,\ldots,n-10,…,n−1, whereas the annuity-immediate pays at 1,…,n1,\ldots,n1,…,n.
