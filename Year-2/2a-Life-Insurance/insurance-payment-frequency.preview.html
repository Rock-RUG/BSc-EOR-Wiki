Death Benefits Payable at Fractional-Year Boundaries¶

Definition¶

A unit death benefit payable at the end of the 1/m1/m1/m-year interval of death is paid at τm=⌈mTx⌉/m\tau_m=\lceil mT_x\rceil/mτm​=⌈mTx​⌉/m, for a positive integer mmm. Its APV is Ax(m)=E[vτm]A_x^{(m)}=\mathbb E[v^{\tau_m}]Ax(m)​=E[vτm​]. Mortality is continuous, so death exactly on a payment boundary has probability zero.

Interpretation¶

Increasing settlement frequency reduces the waiting time after death. Unlike an annuity payable mmm times a year, this is one benefit payment, not mmm instalments of 1/m1/m1/m.
