Prospective Policy Value¶

Definition¶

The prospective gross policy value at duration ttt is Vt=E[Ltg∣policy in force at t]V_t=\mathbb E[L_t^g\mid\text{policy in force at }t]Vt​=E[Ltg​∣policy in force at t]. Equivalently, it is expected time-ttt PV of future benefits and expenses minus expected time-ttt PV of future premiums, under the selected valuation basis and timing convention.

Interpretation¶

The value measures the expected remaining liability of an active policy. It changes as the insured ages and as benefits and premium payments approach or pass.

Key Results¶

  • Vt+E[Yt∣in force]=E[Zt+Ht∣in force]V_t+\mathbb E[Y_t\mid\text{in force}]=\mathbb E[Z_t+H_t\mid\text{in force}]Vt​+E[Yt​∣in force]=E[Zt​+Ht​∣in force].
  • Net policy values omit expenses and use the corresponding net premium.
  • A value at issue is zero under matching equivalence assumptions; this need not hold at later durations or after a change of basis.
  • An expected-liability value is not, by itself, a complete capital requirement for adverse outcomes.

Examples¶

If expected remaining benefits and expenses are 50 and expected remaining premiums are 30, the prospective policy value is 20 at the specified valuation time.
