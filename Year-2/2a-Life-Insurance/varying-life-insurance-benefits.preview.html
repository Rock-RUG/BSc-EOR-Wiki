Varying Life Insurance Benefits¶

Definition¶

A varying death benefit is specified by a deterministic payment rule b(t)≥0b(t)\ge0b(t)≥0. A compound reversionary bonus increases the insured benefit by a fixed proportion at specified vesting dates.

Interpretation¶

The mortality model determines whether and when payment occurs; the benefit rule determines its amount. Write the vesting convention explicitly before valuing a bonus.
