Select and Ultimate Mortality¶

Definition¶

A select mortality model records both age at selection and elapsed duration since selection. Write q[x]+sq_{[x]+s}q[x]+s​ for the one-year death probability of a life selected at age xxx and observed sss years later. After a specified select period rrr, the ultimate rate depends only on attained age: q[x]+s=qx+sq_{[x]+s}=q_{x+s}q[x]+s​=qx+s​ for s≥rs\ge rs≥r.

Interpretation¶

Underwriting can make newly selected lives differ from other lives of the same age. The model keeps that duration effect until the assumed select period ends.
