Definition¶

Interpretation¶

The policy combines a state trigger with a clock. The (s,S)(s,S)(s,S) policy orders as soon as the position is low but may wait a long time when demand is low; the (T,S)(T,S)(T,S) policy orders on a fixed schedule even when the position has fallen far. Under (T,s,S)(T,s,S)(T,s,S) a cycle ends at whichever trigger fires first, so no cycle lasts longer than TTT periods.
