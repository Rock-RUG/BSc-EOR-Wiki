Interpretation¶

The inventory level says what is on the shelf, or owed to customers, now; the inventory position says what that will become once every order already placed has arrived. An order placed today arrives after lll periods, when the orders already on the way have arrived too, so the decision to order must count them.

Each period shows its start value on the left half and its end value on the right half; demand moves both staircases down equally. The order Q4=6Q_4=6Q4​=6 lifts the dashed position to S=8S=8S=8 at the start of period 444, but the solid level only when the order arrives, at the start of period 666. In period 555 the gap of 666 units is the quantity on order, while the level is already −2-2−2.

The two update rules differ in one index. Placing an order raises PPP at once; receiving it raises III and leaves PPP unchanged, because the units only move from "on order" to "on hand". With l=0l=0l=0 nothing is ever outstanding and Pk=IkP_k=I_kPk​=Ik​. A backlog enters with a minus sign, so the position may be negative.

In EOQ with lead times the trigger was on-hand stock, which needs the effective lead time once the delay exceeds a cycle; a trigger on the position already counts the overlapping orders. The common slip is to decide on the level, which sees an order only lll periods late and orders the same units twice (Example 3). Hence the base-stock, (s,S)(s,S)(s,S), (Q,r)(Q,r)(Q,r) and (T,S)(T,S)(T,S) policies all read the position.

  • Under the four policies of Section 2.5 the order QkQ_kQk​ depends only on Pk−1′P_{k-1}'Pk−1′​ (and on kkk for the (T,S)(T,S)(T,S) policy), so the path of the position does not depend on the lead time; the lead time only shifts when the orders reach the inventory level.
  • Under a base-stock policy, Pk=SP_k=SPk​=S for all kkk, and the inventory level is SSS minus the demand of the last lll periods; see Base-Stock Inventory Distribution (Week 5).
  • Under a (Q,r)(Q,r)(Q,r) policy, the position stays in {r+1,…,r+Q}\{r+1,\dots,r+Q\}{r+1,…,r+Q} and is uniformly distributed there in the long run; see Uniform Inventory Position under a (Q, r) Policy (Week 5).
