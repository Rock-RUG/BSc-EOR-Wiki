Base-Stock Inventory Distribution¶

Theorem¶

Interpretation¶

A base-stock policy reorders every unit as soon as it is demanded, so the inventory position is always SSS. Everything that was ordered more than lll periods ago has arrived; what is missing from SSS is exactly the demand of the last lll periods. The inventory level is therefore SSS minus a fresh copy of the lead-time demand, and its pmf is the pmf of XXX mirrored and shifted so that X=0X=0X=0 sits at SSS.
