Economic Production Quantity¶

Definition¶

The economic production quantity (EPQ) model is a single-item inventory system in which demand arrives at a constant rate ddd and a machine, when switched on, produces at a constant rate r≥dr\ge dr≥d. The inventory level I(t)I(t)I(t) is controlled by an (s,S)(s,S)(s,S) policy with zero lead time: when I(t)I(t)I(t) falls to sss the machine is switched on, at setup cost KKK, and when I(t)I(t)I(t) reaches SSS it is switched off. Unmet demand is backlogged. Holding costs hhh per unit per unit time are charged on positive inventory and backlogging costs bbb per unit per unit time on negative inventory. Interpretation¶

The EPQ model is the economic order quantity model with two realistic changes: replenishment comes from a machine that needs time to produce, and planned backlog is allowed. Every cycle looks the same, so the renewal reward theorem reduces the long-run cost rate to one cycle's cost divided by its length.
