Closed Queueing Network¶

Definition¶

A closed queueing network consists of mmm stations and a fixed number nnn of jobs that circulate among them: no job arrives from outside and none leaves. After service at station iii a job moves to station jjj with probability PijP_{ij}Pij​, where ∑j=1mPij=1\displaystyle \sum_{j=1}^mP_{ij}=1j=1∑m​Pij​=1 for every iii, or it follows a fixed route. With Li(t)L_i(t)Li​(t) the number of jobs at station iii, including any job in service,

L1(t)+L2(t)+⋯+Lm(t)=nfor all t.L_1(t)+L_2(t)+\cdots+L_m(t)=n\quad\text{for all }t.L1​(t)+L2​(t)+⋯+Lm​(t)=nfor all t.

The model of Section 7.5 has single-server, first-come-first-served stations with exponential service times SiS_iSi​ of mean E[Si]\mathbb{E}[S_i]E[Si​]. Station 1 serves as the reference station: ViV_iVi​ is the mean number of visits to station iii per visit to station 1 (the visit ratio, V1=1V_1=1V1​=1). Performance measures depend on the population nnn: the stationary mean number E[Li(n)]\mathbb{E}[L_i(n)]E[Li​(n)] at station iii, the mean time E[Ji(n)]\mathbb{E}[J_i(n)]E[Ji​(n)] per visit there, the throughput THi(n)\mathrm{TH}_i(n)THi​(n), and the mean cycle time E[J(n)]\mathbb{E}[J(n)]E[J(n)] from a visit to station 1 to the next.

Interpretation¶

In an open network the arrival rate is given and we ask how much congestion it causes. In a closed network the number of jobs is given, and the throughput is the result: the stations pass a fixed set of jobs around, and the speed of that circulation is what we want to know.

The figure shows the two-station cycle of Example 1 with n=3n=3n=3 jobs, E[S1]=2\mathbb{E}[S_1]=2E[S1​]=2 and E[S2]=1\mathbb{E}[S_2]=1E[S2​]=1. Since L2=3−L1L_2=3-L_1L2​=3−L1​, the state is k=L1k=L_1k=L1​ alone. A service completion at station 2 moves a job to station 1 (blue, k→k+1k\to k+1k→k+1, rate μ2=1\mu_2=1μ2​=1); one at station 1 moves it back (red, rate μ1=12\mu_1=\tfrac{1}{2}μ1​=21​). The stationary probabilities underneath double from state to state, because station 1 is the slower one.

Since no job enters from outside, nothing can pile up without bound: the utilizations THi(n)E[Si]\mathrm{TH}_i(n)\mathbb{E}[S_i]THi​(n)E[Si​] stay below one for every nnn, and there is no stability condition. What adding jobs does is push the throughput up towards the capacity of the bottleneck. The station states are dependent, as they must sum to nnn, so the stations cannot be treated as separate M/M/1M/M/1M/M/1 queues (Example 3).

The two-station cycle is a birth-death chain, solved by the level-crossing equations of Week 4; the machine-repair model of the finite calling population is a closed system of the same kind. For more stations one uses mean value analysis, and a CONWIP production line is a closed network in which cards fix nnn.
